How a deal moves

One thread from first request to final payment — every stage is recorded in Biddex. Payments go directly between the companies and are confirmed on the platform.

01

RFQ

Buyer posts a request with quantities and specs. A budget is optional.

02

Quotes

Verified suppliers submit priced quotes.

03

LPO

Buyer awards one quote; a local purchase order is issued.

04

Order

Supplier confirms and prepares the order against the LPO.

05

Delivery

Fulfilment is tracked against the agreed terms.

06

Invoice

The invoice is created when the supplier accepts the LPO; it becomes payable once the buyer confirms receipt.

07

Payment

After receipt, the buyer pays the supplier directly and records it; the supplier confirms the payment.

Verification is the product

A marketplace is only as useful as the trust it can vouch for. Biddex doesn't take a company's word for it — every account is reviewed by our team before it can post an RFQ or submit a quote.

01

Documents submitted

The company uploads its trade license or commercial registration (CR) certificate.

02

Reviewed by the Biddex team

Our team checks the document by hand before the company can post an RFQ or submit a quote.

03

Re-checked on changes

If a verified company changes its name or CR number, it goes back to review before it can trade again.
