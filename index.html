How a deal moves

One thread from first request to final payment — every stage stays inside Biddex, so nothing gets negotiated off-platform where it can't be tracked.

01

RFQ

Buyer posts a request with quantities, specs and a budget.

02

Quotes

Verified suppliers in the category submit priced quotes.

03

LPO

Buyer awards one quote; a local purchase order is issued.

04

Order

Supplier confirms and prepares the order against the LPO.

05

Delivery

Fulfilment is tracked against the agreed terms.

06

Invoice

Supplier invoices against the delivered order.

07

Payment

Buyer settles the invoice and the record closes out.

Verification is the product

A marketplace is only as useful as the trust it can vouch for. Biddex doesn't take a company's word for it — every account clears checks against the official registry before it can transact.

01

Registration confirmed

Company details are matched against the Sijillat commercial registry, not typed into a form and trusted.

02

Standing checked

An account only reaches full access once its registration is confirmed active and in good standing.

03

Re-cleared annually

The check runs again every year, so a supplier can't go quiet on compliance after their first approval.
